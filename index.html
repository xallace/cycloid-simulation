Wankel Rotary Engine Kinematics

The housing wall of a Wankel rotary engine is a two-lobed epitrochoid. The triangular rotor orbits on an eccentric shaft with eccentricity $e$ and rotates via an internal ring gear at exactly 1/3 the speed of the eccentric shaft. As a result, all three apex seals maintain continuous tangential contact with the trochoid wall at every shaft angle.

🦾

Cycloidal Speed Reducers (Robotics)

Modern industrial robots (e.g., from KUKA or Fanuc) rely on cycloidal speed reducers (Cyclo Drives). A cycloidal disc with $N$ lobes rolls inside a stationary ring of $N+1$ roller pins. The advantage: extremely high reduction ratios ($i = 10:1$ to $100:1$ in a single stage) with zero backlash and high shock-load capacity.
